# Mahila-Mitra

Research project exploring existing AI methods for ovarian ultrasound and related PCOS datasets.

## Objectives
- Study three existing datasets and their associated research papers.
- Reproduce documented preprocessing and feature-extraction steps.
- Evaluate methods where supported by the papers and available data.
- Compare our experimental results with published findings.

## Research integrity
This project is for educational and research purposes, not clinical diagnosis.
Published results will be distinguished from results obtained in this project.

## Status
Initial setup. Dataset and research-paper mapping is in progress.
